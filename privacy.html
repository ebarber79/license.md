Neon Dash: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Neon Dash is an endless-runner game. You can play it in a web browser or as an Android app from Google Play. This policy explains what data the game and the services it uses handle, and the choices you have. There are no accounts or logins, and the game has no server of its own.

Progress saved on your device

Your best score, gems, unlocked skins and shoes, daily streak, missions and settings (sound, haptics, reduced motion) are saved only on your device: in your browser's storage on the web, or in the app's storage on Android. This data is not sent to us. Clearing the browser's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Ads in the Android app (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch for a revive, double gems or a cosmetic unlock. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (such as model, operating system and app version),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • how you interact with ads.

Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

Your consent choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or another region that requires it, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads load. If consent rules out ads, the app shows none and hides the watch-an-ad buttons. When the form applies to you, a Privacy choices button on the start screen lets you change your choice at any time.

Purchases in the Android app

The app may offer optional gem packs. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play, so we never see your card or payment details. Purchases are checked and delivered through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app ID that RevenueCat creates, your purchase records from Google Play (product, time, transaction ID) and basic device information. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

The web version

The browser version on GitHub Pages shows no ads and sends no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address and browser details as part of serving the page; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you play Neon Dash on a third-party game portal, that portal runs its own ads and analytics under its own privacy policy.

Children

Neon Dash is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. Ad requests are marked as not child-directed and are limited to content suitable for a general teen audience. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Keeping and deleting data

Progress on your device stays until you clear it or uninstall the app. Google and RevenueCat keep the data they receive under their own retention policies. To ask about deleting data connected to your purchases, email us with your Google Play order number.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
