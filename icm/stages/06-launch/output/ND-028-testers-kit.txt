NEON DASH: TESTER RECRUITMENT KIT
=================================

Why this matters: Google requires new personal developer accounts to run a CLOSED TEST
with at least 12 testers who stay opted in for 14 days in a row before the game can go
public. The 14-day clock starts when the test starts, so line people up now.

Aim for 15-20 people. Some will forget to opt in or will uninstall.
Each tester needs: an Android phone + the Google (Gmail) account they use for the Play Store.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hey! I made an Android game, Neon Dash (neon runner, tap to jump). Google needs 12 people

(Android phones only. Just keep it installed for 14 days.)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP A. Create the tester group (you, once, 3 minutes)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Google Group is the tester list. Play Console takes the group's address, so anyone who
joins the group is a tester. No typing emails into Play one by one.

1. On a computer, open https://groups.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
2. Click "Create group".
3. Group name: Neon Dash Testers
   Group email: neondash-testers  (if taken, try neondash-beta or neondashtesters)
   Description: Testers for the Neon Dash Android game.
4. Privacy settings:
   - Who can search for group: Anyone on the web
   - Who can join group: Anyone can join
   - Who can view conversations: Group managers
   - Who can post: Group managers
   - Who can view members: Group managers
5. Skip adding members. Click "Create group".
6. Your join link is:  https://groups.google.com/g/<group-email-name>
   (for example https://groups.google.com/g/neondash-testers)
   Send that link with the invite below.
7. Tell your terminal agent the group email, so it goes into Play Console later.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP C. The test is OPEN (approved 2026-11-04). Send this right after they join the group.
(For new people you can now send A/B and C together: join the group, then this link.)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Neon Dash test is open! On your Android phone:
1. Open this link: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/io.github.ebarber79.neondash
2. Tap "Become a tester".
3. Tap "Download it on Google Play" and install.
4. Please keep it installed for 14 days, and don't tap "Leave the program". Open it now
   and then if you can.
It may say "Early access" or "Unreleased" on the Play Store. That's normal.
If the link says you can't join, make sure you're signed in with the same Google account
you used to join the group.
