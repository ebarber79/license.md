NEON DASH: PLAN TO GET 12+ TESTERS (AND PASS GOOGLE'S PRODUCTION REVIEW)
=========================================================================
Written Nov 5, 2026. Everyone signs up through ONE link:
    https://neondash.gridlas.com/test.html

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE RULE YOU HAVE TO MEET
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 12 or more testers opted in to the closed test, for 14 days IN A ROW.
  The 14-day clock only runs while you're at 12+. If you drop to 11, it stops.
- After that, Play Console asks you questions before granting production
  (how you found testers, what feedback you got, what you changed).
  Apps get rejected when testers signed up but never opened the app.
  So you need testers who PLAY a bit, plus a record of feedback.

TARGET: 20 sign-ups, not 12. People drop out or forget.
DEADLINE: 12 testers by about Nov 14, so the 14 days finish around Nov 28,
then apply for production (Google's review is usually a few days to a week).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHERE TESTERS COME FROM (in order of how well it works)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PEOPLE YOU KNOW (aim: 8 to 10). Best by far: they stay 14 days and give
   honest feedback.
   - Make a list now: family, friends, coworkers, old classmates, neighbours,
     group chats. Only Android users count (iPhone can't join).
   - Send the text below one person at a time. Personal texts beat a group blast.
   - Ask each yes to forward it to one more Android friend.

2. TESTER-SWAP COMMUNITIES (aim: 6 to 10). Other indie developers stuck on the
   same 12-tester rule. You test theirs, they test yours.
   - Reddit: r/AndroidClosedTesting and r/TestersCommunity (read each sub's
     rules before posting; most want a swap offer in the post).
   - Search Discord for "closed testing" / "12 testers" swap servers.
   - The catch: you must actually install their apps and keep them for 14 days.
     Budget 2 minutes a day. Keep a list so you keep your side of the deal.

3. YOUR OWN CHANNELS (aim: a few, plus feedback).
   - Post on any social accounts you use (Facebook, Instagram story, X,
     Threads, a Discord you're in). Use the social post below.
   - Ask for testers in hobby groups you already belong to (gaming, local).

4. PAID TESTER SERVICES: last resort only. They exist and many developers use
   them, but the testers rarely play, which is exactly what gets production
   requests rejected. Try 1 to 3 first.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEEP THEM PLAYING (this is what passes the review)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Day 1: thank them. Tell them the one rule: keep it installed 14 days.
- About day 5 and day 10: a short nudge (text below). Ask for ONE piece of
  feedback: "what was annoying or confusing?"
- Write every piece of feedback in the log at the bottom of this file, with
  the date. Paste them to me and I'll fix what makes sense in the next update.
  These notes become your answers on the production form.
- Shipping v6 (Back button + portrait lock) during the test is good: it shows
  Google you acted on feedback.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO COUNT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Group members: https://groups.google.com/g/neondash-testers -> Members.
  Joining the group is step 1 only; they also need step 2 ("Become a tester").
- Play Console Dashboard shows the production-access card with the opted-in
  count once Google tracks it. Ask me "check testers" and I'll read it for you.
