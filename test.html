Help test Neon Dash

Neon Dash is a fast neon runner: tap to jump over spikes. Before Google lets a new game onto the Play Store, at least 12 people have to test it for 14 days. It's free and takes about 2 minutes.

Android phones only. Use the same Google account in both steps: the one signed in to the Play Store on your phone.

Step 1: Join the tester group

Opens Google Groups. Tap Join group. You won't get any emails from it.

Join the tester group

Step 2: Become a tester and install

Tap Become a tester, then Download it on Google Play and install.

Become a tester

Then just keep it installed for 14 days

  • Playing is up to you, but opening it now and then helps.
  • Please don't uninstall it or tap "Leave the program" for 14 days.
  • The Play Store may say "Early access" or "Unreleased". That's normal.

If something goes wrong

  • "You can't join" or "App not available": you probably joined the group with a different Google account. Do step 1 again with the account on your phone, wait a minute, then step 2.
  • No iPhone version yet. You can still play in your browser: play Neon Dash.
  • Questions or bugs: neondash@gridlas.com
