## Long description
> Sprint through a glowing neon world in this fast, one-more-run endless runner.
> Tap to jump and double-jump over spikes and bars, scoop up gems, and grab
> power-ups — a shield to take a hit, a magnet to vacuum gems, and slow-mo to
> thread the tight spots. The longer you survive, the faster it gets and the
> deeper the colors shift.
>
> Spend your gems in the shop on six unlockable skins (including an animated
> rainbow), keep a daily streak alive, clear daily challenges and missions, and
> chase a new best score every run.
>
> • Easy to learn, tough to master — instant restarts
> • Power-ups: shield, magnet, slow-mo
> • Unlockable skins + daily rewards & missions
> • Smooth on phone and desktop, plays in any browser

## Controls / instructions
- **Tap / click / Space** — jump. **Tap again in mid-air** — double jump.
- Dodge red spikes & pink bars, collect gold gems, grab power-ups.
