NEON DASH v5 (1.4): PHONE TEST CHECKLIST
========================================
About 15 minutes. Use your own Android phone, with the game installed FROM GOOGLE PLAY
(not a sideloaded APK). Mark each line [x] pass, [!] fail, or [-] couldn't check.
For every [!], write one line about what you saw. A screenshot helps (save it to Drive).

ADS RULE: watch ads all the way through, but NEVER tap on an ad itself (tapping your own
live ads can get the AdMob account banned). Closing an ad with its X is fine.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. INSTALL
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] On the phone, opened https://neondash.gridlas.com/test.html and did Step 1 (Join group)
    and Step 2 (Become a tester) with the SAME Google account as the phone's Play Store
[ ] Installed from the Play Store page (it may say "Early access" / "Unreleased"; that's normal)
[ ] Play Store app page shows version 1.4 (or the app's Update button got you there)
    Note: if you still have an older sideloaded Neon Dash, uninstall it first (Play can't
    update an app signed differently).
[ ] The test page itself: both buttons worked, text was clear (note anything confusing)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ICON + LAUNCH (ND-013)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Home-screen icon is the Neon Dash neon art on a dark purple background
    (NOT the blue Capacitor "X" logo), not cropped or squashed
[ ] Splash screen on launch is Neon Dash art, not the Capacitor logo
[ ] Game opens to the start screen within a few seconds

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. START SCREEN FITS (ND-020, ND-019)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] The NEON DASH title is fully visible (not cut off at the top)
[ ] PLAY button visible without scrolling
[ ] If anything is below the screen edge, you can scroll the menu to reach it
[ ] NO "Scan to play on your phone" QR code on the start screen (it should be app-free)
[ ] (Probably won't show in the US) If a "Privacy choices" button appears, it's tappable.
    It normally only shows in the EU/UK. Mark [-] if you don't see it.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. MUSIC + SOUND (ND-025)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Music plays during a run (synthwave), and gets more intense as you speed up
[ ] The Music button turns music off/on without killing sound effects
[ ] The Sound button turns jump/gem effects off/on without killing music
[ ] Your choices are remembered after closing and reopening the app
[ ] Music stops when you switch to another app or lock the phone, and doesn't play over
    the top of ads

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. GAMEPLAY + SHOES (ND-021)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Tap = jump; tap again in the air = double jump; again = triple jump
[ ] Picking up a power-up makes the shoes glow (each power-up its own colour)
[ ] Two power-ups at once = rainbow shoes
[ ] Shoes blink just before a power-up runs out
[ ] Game runs smoothly (no obvious stutter or freezing)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. GAME-OVER SCREEN + REWARDED ADS (ND-020, ND-022)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Game-over screen fits: score, buttons and MENU visible; sound button doesn't cover MENU
[ ] "▶ CONTINUE · watch ad" appears. Watch the ad to the end: you continue the same run
[ ] "🎬 DOUBLE GEMS" appears. Watch the ad: gems from that run double
[ ] Close an ad EARLY (if it allows): you do NOT get the reward (that's correct)
[ ] Occasional full-screen ad between runs (about every 3rd replay, not every run)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. BOOST (ND-023): never tested on a real phone yet
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Start (or game-over) screen shows a Boost panel with Shield 🛡, Magnet 🧲, Slow-mo ⏱
[ ] Pick one and use the WATCH-AD option. Watch to the end: the next run starts with that
    power-up (shoes glow right away)
[ ] Right after a Boost ad, the next replay does NOT also show a full-screen ad
[ ] Pay-with-gems option (40 gems): works if you have 40+ gems, gems go down by 40,
    and it's refused (not charged) if you have fewer
[ ] A paid Boost survives closing and reopening the app before the run

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. GEMS + PROGRESS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Gems collected in a run are added to your total
[ ] Close the app fully (swipe it away), reopen: gems, best score and unlocks are still there
[ ] The shop shows 4 gem packs with prices: Handful 500, Pouch 1,200, Chest 3,000,
    Vault 7,000 (prices in your currency, about $0.99 / $1.99 / $4.99 / $9.99)
[ ] TEST PURCHASE (best tomorrow, Nov 5 or later): buy the cheapest pack. Google's sheet
    should say it's a test / "Test card, always approves" and you are NOT charged.
    -> gems go up by 500 right away
[ ] Close the app fully and reopen: the 500 bought gems are still there
[ ] Start a purchase and cancel it: no gems added, no error message, game still works
    If any purchase step fails, write the exact message you saw.

8. GENERAL
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Rotate the phone to landscape and back: note what happens (the lock comes in v6)
